Friendly House plants

1. Golden Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)

Description: A classic, vining plant with heart-shaped leaves, often variegated with cream or yellow. It is incredibly hardy, forgiving of missed waterings, and grows well in trailing baskets or on shelves.  Best For: Hanging planters, bookshelves, or on top of cabinets.  Light/Water: Thrives in low to bright indirect light; water when top 2 inches of soil are dry.



Snake Plant (Dracaena trifasciata)

Description: A tough, architectural plant with tall, sword-like leaves that grow vertically. It features, deep-green bands and is exceptionally low-maintenance.  Best For: Office spaces, corners, or as a modern decorative element.  Light/Water: Tolerates low light but prefers bright indirect; water sparingly (only when soil is completely dry).



3. ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)

Description: Known for its thick, waxy, deep-green leaves, this plant looks almost fake due to its high gloss. It is extremely drought-tolerant and adapts to almost any light condition.  Best For: Dark corners, offices, or forgetful owners.  Light/Water: Thrives in low to bright indirect light; water when the soil is nearly dry.



4. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)

Description: A popular plant with long, slender, arching leaves that often produce small "plantlets" or babies that dangle down.  Best For: Hanging planters or high shelves.  Light/Water: Thrives in indirect light; keep soil lightly moist.



5. Heartleaf Philodendron (Philodendron hederaceum)

Description: A fast-growing vining plant with deep green, heart-shaped leaves. It is very adaptable, loves to climb or trail, and is very forgiving of inconsistent care.  Best For: Shelves, trailing down furniture, or on a moss pole.  Light/Water: Prefers bright, indirect light but adapts to lower light; water when the top inch of soil is dry.
